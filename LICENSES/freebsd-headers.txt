FreeBSD headers read by scripts/generate_freebsd_types.py.  The FreeBSD type
libraries, lib/types/headers/FreeBSDX64.h, FreeBSDX86.h, FreeBSDARM64.h and
FreeBSDARM.h, are rendered from the headers of FreeBSD 15.1-RELEASE as its
base.txz installs them in /usr/include, and hold only what NeverD reads from
them and renders again: the layouts of data structures, the values of
enumerations and the declarations of functions.  The notices of the headers
read follow, each license once after the copyright lines of every header that
states it.
